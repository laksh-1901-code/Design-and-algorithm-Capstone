Benchmark

Random sparse timetables, 6 to 18 subjects. Bars show backtracking nodes explored.

Complexity. Backtracking: O(kⁿ) worst case time, O(n) space. First-fit greedy: O(n + m). Graph k-colouring is NP-complete for k ≥ 3, so pruning (degree ordering and symmetry breaking) is what keeps typical timetables fast.
